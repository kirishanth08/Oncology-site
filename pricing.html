Regarding Chemotherapy & Infusion Treatment Costs

Because cancer therapeutics vary widely based on biomarker profiling, dosage protocols, supportive pre-medications, and individual health plans, OncoAid does not present simplistic package pricing for chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or targeted biologics.

Contact the care team for treatment-specific information. Our dedicated financial counselors review your precise clinical plan and insurance benefits before any therapy begins.

Insurance Advocacy

NAVIGATING INSURANCE PRE-AUTHORIZATIONS

We handle the paperwork and phone calls with your insurer so you don't have to navigate coverage appeals alone.

Coverage Verification

We verify in-network coverage, deductible status, and out-of-pocket maximum thresholds prior to appointments.

Prior Authorization Filing

Our clinical staff submits detailed medical necessity documentation directly to your insurance company for infusions and imaging.

Denial & Peer Appeals

If an insurer hesitates on a prescribed drug, our medical oncologists conduct direct peer-to-peer discussions to advocate for your care.

Patient Foundations

CO-PAY RELIEF & COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Many patients qualify for independent non-profit foundation grants and manufacturer patient assistance programs (PAPs) to offset costly copayments for specialty oncology oral pills and infusions.

Non-Profit Disease Funds Assistance with transportation, premiums, and out-of-pocket drug costs.
Hospital Compassion Discounts Sliding-scale fee adjustments based on household income for uninsured patients.
Grant Support

NON-PROFIT CO-PAY ASSISTANCE & GRANTS

Cancer treatments should never cause financial ruin. Our team matches patients with external philanthropic foundations and copay assistance programs.

Foundation Grant Matching

We monitor daily open funds across the PAN Foundation, HealthWell Foundation, and Leukemia & Lymphoma Society to secure co-pay grants.

Manufacturer Patient Assistance

For high-cost oral targeted drugs or biologics, our coordinators apply directly to manufacturer compassion programs for zero or reduced co-pays.

Paperwork Handled For You

We collect the tax forms, doctor attestations, and clinical notes required by grant portals so you never have to navigate bureaucratic red tape.

Honest Billing

OUR NO-SURPRISE-BILLING COMMITMENT

We believe financial predictability is an essential component of clinical comfort. We pledge complete transparency before your first appointment.

Written Itemized Good Faith Estimates You receive an upfront written estimate detailing physician consults, lab draws, and facility components.
