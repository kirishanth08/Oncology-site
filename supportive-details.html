Supportive Pathway

STEP-BY-STEP SUPPORTIVE CARE ROADMAP

From baseline assessment to post-session pacing, your supportive therapy is seamlessly interwoven with your medical oncologist's treatment plan.

Phase 01 Intake
Baseline Symptom & Lab Audit

We review current chemotherapy/radiation cycles, blood cell nadirs, organ markers, and symptom logs.

Phase 02 Alignment
Oncologist Clearance

Direct coordination with your primary oncologist ensures zero drug interactions or physical contraindications.

Phase 03 Session
Chairside or Studio Delivery

Administered in private healing lounges, quiet consultation suites, or bedside during infusion hours.

Phase 04 Ongoing
Home Pacing & Family Check-Ins

Continuous symptom tracking, home routine guides, and scheduled check-ins between medical cycles.

Oncologist-Approved Protocols Every supportive modality is cleared by your tumor board to prevent interference with systemic agents.
Credentialed Oncology Specialists Delivered exclusively by Board-Certified CSO dietitians, OSW-C social workers, and CLT therapists.
Whole-Family Integration Spouses, care partners, and children are welcomed into respite scheduling and counseling circles.
