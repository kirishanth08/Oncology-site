Clinical Governance

HOW OUR MULTIDISCIPLINARY TUMOR BOARD WORKS

Cancer is never managed in isolation. Every patient benefits from the collective intellect of multiple oncology subspecialists sitting in consensus review.

1. Slide & Scan Re-Review

Biopsy pathology slides and CT/PET imaging are independently validated by our diagnostic team prior to the board convening.

2. Multi-Specialist Debate

Medical oncologists, radiation oncologists, and surgical partners evaluate sequencing options according to NCCN benchmarks.

3. Consensus Blueprint

A unified, written clinical recommendation is produced, outlining curative intent, supportive therapies, and scheduled milestones.

4. Patient Delivery

Your primary oncologist and assigned nurse navigator sit down with you and your family to explain every finding in plain, reassuring language.
