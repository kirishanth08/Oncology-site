01 Initial Meeting

Diagnostic Review & Consultation

Unhurried 60+ minute dialogue reviewing pathology, imaging, personal goals, and immediate family questions with your oncologist.

Result: Printed roadmap & navigator assigned
02 Tumor Board

Multidisciplinary Care Planning

Genomic biomarkers and staging are reviewed by our multi-specialist team to craft your tailored targeted therapy or immunotherapy plan.

Result: Pre-authorization check & cycle schedule
03 Active Phase

Active Treatment Coordination

Precision infusions in sunlit private suites. Blood counts checked 24h prior, tailored anti-nausea premeds, and chair-side comfort.

Result: 24/7 care desk access & follow-up calls
04 Integrative

Ongoing Monitoring & Integrative Care

Active symptom management between cycles: appetite support, hydration planning, taste restoration dietetics, and caregiver respite.

Result: Tailored nutrition templates & family support circles
05 Long-Term

Follow-Up & Survivorship Passport

Transitioning into remission surveillance. Regular scan intervals, late-effect monitoring, and physical vitality rebuilding goals.

Result: Complete survivorship passport & checkup cadence

Preparation Concierge

WHAT TO PREPARE BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Arriving well-prepared helps our oncology team evaluate your case without delays and ensures all your critical questions are thoroughly addressed:

Original Imaging Discs & Reports Bring official DICOM CDs/DVDs of your CT, PET, or MRI scans and biopsy pathology slides from referring clinics.
Full Medication & Supplement Inventory An up-to-date log of all current prescription medicines, over-the-counter drugs, vitamins, and dosages.
Insurance Cards & Photo Identification Primary and secondary medical insurance cards, prescription benefit information, and a valid photo ID.
Key Questions for Your Oncologist:
• “What is the exact biological subtype of this cancer and its targeted therapy vulnerabilities?”
• “What are expected side effects in the first 48 hours, and how do we prevent them?”
• “Who is our direct 24/7 triage nurse contact if a fever above 100.4°F develops?”
