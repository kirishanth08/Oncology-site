Medical Literacy

ONCOLOGY VOCABULARY DEMYSTIFIED

Understanding the vocabulary on your pathology report gives you control and confidence during consultations.

Genomics
Biomarker / Mutation

Specific biological traits in DNA or proteins (such as HER2 or EGFR) that indicate which targeted therapies will be most effective.

Timing
Neoadjuvant vs Adjuvant

Neoadjuvant therapy is delivered before surgery to shrink tumors; adjuvant therapy is given after surgery to destroy stray cells.

Surveillance
Liquid Biopsy (ctDNA)

A simple blood test detecting circulating tumor DNA to monitor treatment response months earlier than traditional scans.

Outcome
Complete Remission

The complete disappearance of all evidence of cancer on physical exams, scans, and blood counts following therapeutic protocols.
