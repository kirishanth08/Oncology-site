The OncoAid Framework

YOUR CARE JOURNEY, CLEARLY EXPLAINED.

We understand that receiving a cancer diagnosis or starting a new phase of care can feel overwhelming. Here is how we walk with you through every milestone.

01
Initial Consultation

Comprehensive review of prior records, pathology, and imaging in a calm, unpressured dialogue.

02
Care Planning

Multidisciplinary tumor board review to assemble your customized evidence-based treatment blueprint.

03
Treatment Coordination

Active scheduling, infusion suite setup, supportive medication prep, and nurse navigation.

04
Ongoing Support

Continuous symptom tracking, nutritional coaching, emotional counseling, and family caregiver check-ins.

05
Follow-Up Care

Structured survivorship surveillance, recurrence screening, and steady physical restoration.

Quality Standards

MULTI-DISCIPLINARY CLINICAL GOVERNANCE

Our protocols follow verified international benchmarks to ensure safety, diagnostic precision, and therapeutic efficacy.

NCCN Guideline Alignment

Every chemotherapy, immunotherapy, and targeted therapy protocol complies with the latest National Comprehensive Cancer Network criteria.

CAP-Certified Pathology

All molecular biomarker sequencing and biopsy reviews are processed through College of American Pathologists certified laboratories.

ASCO QOPI Standards

Clinical practice measures adhere to American Society of Clinical Oncology Quality Oncology Practice Initiative benchmarks.

Weekly Board Consensus

No complex treatment begins without multi-specialist tumor board consensus across oncologists, radiologists, and nurses.
