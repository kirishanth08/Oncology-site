Preparation Guidance

WHAT TO PREPARE BEFORE YOUR FIRST VISIT

Gathering these essentials helps your oncologist spend more time discussing your personalized treatment strategy and less time hunting down paperwork.

Imaging & Scan Discs

Request digital CD copies or portal transfer links of your recent CT, PET, MRI, or mammogram images from previous imaging centers.

Pathology Reports

Any biopsy, surgical pathology, or biomarker test results (such as HER2, ER/PR, EGFR, KRAS, PD-L1, or BRCA testing).

Current Medication List

Write down all prescription medications, over-the-counter vitamins, and herbal supplements along with exact dosages.

Trusted Companion

Bring a spouse, family member, or trusted friend to take notes, ask questions, and provide calm emotional support.

Patient Respect

OUR UNHURRIED CONSULTATION CHARTER

We protect your consultation from the rushed fifteen-minute slots of standard medicine with four clinical guarantees.

Full 60-Minute New Patient Consults

Your initial appointment will never be cut short. Dr. Carter and our senior oncologists allocate a full hour so every question, scan, and concern is thoroughly explored.

Plain-English Care Roadmaps

Before you leave our office, you will receive a printed summary of today's clinical conclusions written in understandable language, not cryptic medical abbreviations.

Named Nurse Navigator on Day One

You won't leave with a generic hospital call center number. You will receive the direct phone extension and email of your personal oncology nurse coordinator.

Zero Double-Booking Guarantee

We respect your time. Our scheduling policy strictly forbids overlapping patient slots, maintaining a peaceful, uncrowded, and prompt clinic experience.
