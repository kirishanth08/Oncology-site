THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TUMOR BOARD

No single oncologist works alone at OncoAid. For every patient facing complex or refractory cancer, our Multidisciplinary Tumor Board convenes weekly to review imaging, molecular biomarkers, and tissue pathology:

Medical Oncologists & Hematologists

Select targeted drug regimens, systemic immunotherapies, and synergistic combinations.

Surgical & Radiation Oncologists

Evaluate resectability, organ preservation, and precise radiosurgical clearance.

Molecular Pathologists & Geneticists

Decipher somatic genetic mutations and hereditary markers to pinpoint novel drug targets.

SUSTAINING REMISSION BEYOND CLEARANCE

Clearing cancer is a momentous milestone. What follows is a dedicated surveillance and survivorship roadmap designed to safeguard your health and empower long-term vitality.

ctDNA Liquid Biopsy

Ultra-sensitive blood screening tracking circulating tumor DNA to detect potential recurrence months before physical symptoms.

Metabolic Nutrition

Anti-inflammatory oncology diet protocols tailored to restore gut microbiome health, cellular energy, and lean muscle mass.

Fatigue Recovery

Specialized restorative physical therapy designed to reverse post-treatment fatigue, improve sleep, and rebuild confidence.

Emotional Harmony

Supportive psychological counseling addressing scan anxiety, family transitions, and life post-active treatment.
