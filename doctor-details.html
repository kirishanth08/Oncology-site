Background & Focus

A LIFELONG DEVOTION TO COMPASSIONATE SCIENCE

Dr. Jenkins earned her medical degree from Harvard Medical School and completed her advanced hematology/oncology fellowship at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute. She has authored over 25 peer-reviewed papers on biomarker-guided therapeutics and targeted treatments for solid tumors.

At OncoAid, Dr. Jenkins leads our multidisciplinary tumor board and champions the philosophy that scientific rigor must always walk hand-in-hand with active listening, clear explanations, and emotional comfort for patients and families.

“Our goal is to demystify complex medical choices. When patients and their loved ones truly understand the ‘why’ behind their treatment plan, anxiety begins to give way to clarity and confidence.”

— Dr. Sarah Jenkins
