Core Specialty

COORDINATED CARE FOR YOUR TREATMENT JOURNEY.

Undergoing chemotherapy or immunotherapy requires more than an infusion chair. OncoAid provides structured coordination — managing pre-labs, scheduling, premedications, and symptom check-ins with unwavering warmth.

Comprehensive Support

WHAT CHEMOTHERAPY COORDINATION MEANS

We oversee the entire ecosystem surrounding infusion treatments. You never have to worry about who is approving your labs or tracking pharmacy deliveries.

Blood Safety
Laboratory Synchronization

We coordinate complete blood panels and kidney/liver function tests 24-48 hours before each cycle so doctors verify safety prior to infusion day.

Proactive Relief
Pre-Medication Regimens

Calibrated anti-emetic medications, steroids, and antihistamines are administered in careful timing to prevent infusion-related reactions.

Direct Line
Direct Nurse Communication

Access to your specific oncology nurse navigator for side-effect guidance, avoiding generic triage phone queues.

Stress-Free Logistics

ORGANIZING YOUR CALENDAR WITH RESPECT

Multiple cycles over months can complicate family routines and employment. Our scheduling coordinators plan cycles weeks in advance, matching dates with your preferred times and transportation logistics.

Predictable Treatment Cycles Clear multi-week schedules with rest intervals planned upfront.
Caregiver & Ride Alignment Estimated departure times so family members know exactly when to pick you up.
Gentle Rescheduling Assistance If blood counts are low or you feel unwell, we coordinate recovery rest days smoothly.

Always Accessible

PROACTIVE CARE TEAM COMMUNICATION

We reach out to you before you even have to ask. Day-after check-in calls ensure unexpected nausea, chills, or fatigue are addressed immediately.

Day +1 Follow-Up Call

Your nurse navigator calls the morning after infusion to review how you slept and assess gastrointestinal comfort.

Patient Portal Messaging

Send non-urgent questions about diet, hydration, or medications directly to your oncology care team.

24/7 Clinical Triage

Clear instructions and on-call oncology physician guidance if you experience fever (>100.4°F) or acute symptoms.

Restoration

SUPPORT BEYOND THE INFUSION SUITE

Our supportive care specialists partner with you between infusion cycles to preserve appetite, protect blood counts, and rebuild strength.

Hydration & Oral Rinse Protocols

Customized electrolyte formulations and soothing non-alcoholic oral rinses to prevent mucositis and keep your mouth comfortable throughout therapy.

Gentle Fatigue Coaching

Energy pacing techniques and structured rest periods that allow you to engage in cherished daily moments with family without exhaustion.

Dignity in Infusion

CHAIR-SIDE COMFORT & SCALP COOLING TECHNOLOGY

We believe receiving chemotherapy should be as calming and restorative as modern medical architecture allows.

Paxman Scalp Cooling

Clinically proven hypothermic scalp caps that constrict blood vessels to reduce chemotherapy drug delivery to hair follicles, significantly preserving hair.

Heated Ergonomic Recliners

Adjustable warming massage chairs with lumbar support, clean linen blankets, and direct sightlines to our outdoor healing botanical garden.

Peace & Privacy Pods

Noise-dampening acoustic dividers, high-speed Wi-Fi, audio relaxation tracks, and companion seating for your chosen family caregiver.

At-Home Protection

WHEN TO CALL YOUR 24/7 ONCOLOGY DESK

Between infusion cycles, you are never without clinical backup. Our direct nurse triage desk is reachable 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Fever Over 100.4°F (38°C) Never wait or self-medicate a fever during chemotherapy. Call immediately for antibiotic and blood count triage.
Uncontrolled Nausea or Inability to Keep Fluids Down Our triage nurse can coordinate prescription anti-emetic adjustments or same-day clinic IV rehydration.
